### Reviewing chatbot algorithms: methods for intelligent dialogue systems
**Published · 2025** · Human-Intelligent Systems Integration, Springer

Lavlu, Md Tousif Hasan; Hassan, Afnanul; Akhtar, Sanjida; Labiba, Salsa-Bil; Mustafa, Hossen Asiful

This review examines chatbot algorithms, from rule-based and retrieval methods to generative and transformer-based approaches. It discusses dialogue evaluation and challenges such as context, multilingual support, and privacy, connecting with my interests in language models and intelligent dialogue systems.
